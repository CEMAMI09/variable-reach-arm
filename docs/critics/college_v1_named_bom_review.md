# Independent print-first BOM review

The earlier critic's $705.64 position applied to an insufficiently optimized
allowance basket; it was not proof of a minimum price. The subsequent independent
review identified overly restrictive metal-only caps/clamps/pulleys, unproven
$60 driver and $35 brake allowances, and vague feedback/protection entries.

Implemented response: named TMC2209 modules, printed drive/support bodies,
12 V slow-operation candidate power architecture, individually named discrete
protection components and a conditional printed friction/catch-cradle approach.

On re-review of the 59-line $368.47 basket, the critic found no high-priority
arithmetic or naming errors. Shipping/tax and optional reserve totals were
independently checked as $446.55 and $513.53. No obvious voltage mismatch was
identified at the BOM level. This is a bounded candidate-parts review, not a
complete electronics or mechanical approval.

Remaining findings: most prices are estimated commodity pack prices rather than
verified vendor listings; exact shaft/belt quantities depend on compact CAD;
12 V/derated-current running torque, printed friction hold, and dump circuit
capacity remain unverified. Keep all these distinctions in user-facing results.
The full-size fair design and its BOM are separate and unchanged by this review.
